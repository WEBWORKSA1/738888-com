Frequently asked questions

When is Chinese New Year 2027?

Saturday, 6 February 2027 — the start of the Year of the Fire Goat.

What is Double 11?

Singles' Day on 11 November, the largest online shopping event in China, now a multi-week promotion across major platforms.

What is 520 and why does it matter to marketers?

5-2-0 sounds like 'I love you' (我愛你). May 20 has become a major day for gifts, jewellery, flowers and couple promotions.

Which dates should businesses avoid for launches?

Many Chinese customers avoid major launches during the 7th lunar month (Ghost Month) and around Qingming (tomb-sweeping day, around 4-5 April).
