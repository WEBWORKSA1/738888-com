Why it matters

In Chinese markets, numbers are a pricing signal.

The digit 8 (八 bā) sounds like 發 fā — "to prosper". That single homophone moves real money: Sichuan Airlines paid CN¥2.33 million for the phone number +86 28 8888 8888, Hong Kong's plate "88" sold for HK$11.4 million in February 2025, and the Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/2008.

The opposite is true for 4 (四 sì ≈ 死 "death"): buildings skip floors, sellers avoid prices, and numeric domains with a 4 trade at a discount.

Frequently asked questions

What does 738888 mean in Chinese?

Read digit by digit: 7 (七 qī) sounds like 起 'rise', 3 (三 sān / Cantonese saam) sounds like 生 'life, growth', and 8 (八 bā) sounds like 發 'prosper'. Together 738888 reads as 'rise, grow and prosper four times over'.

Are the tools free?

Yes. Every tool on 738888.com is free and runs in your browser. We are funded by advertising, sponsors, supporters and our optional Prosperity Growth Desk services.

Is a lucky number a guarantee of success?

No. Number symbolism is a cultural tradition. It influences customer perception and buyer demand in Chinese-speaking markets, which is why businesses pay attention to it, but it does not guarantee outcomes.

Who is 738888.com for?

Business owners, marketers, real-estate agents, restaurants, e-commerce sellers and families who live in, sell to, or celebrate Chinese culture anywhere in the world.
