Business etiquette with numbers

  • Pricing: end prices in 8 or 88; avoid 4 anywhere (including cents and SKU codes customers see).
  • Gifts: give in pairs; never four items; avoid clocks (送鐘 sounds like attending a funeral) and sharp objects.
  • Floors & units: expect premiums for 8, 18, 28, 88; many buildings skip 4, 13, 14.
  • Dates: avoid Ghost Month and Qingming for grand openings; festival days and dates rich in 8 are preferred.
  • Copy: "38" can be rude in Mandarin slang — context matters; test with native speakers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the luckiest number in Chinese?

Eight (八 bā), because it sounds like 發 fā, to prosper. Six (smooth flow) and nine (long-lasting) are also strongly positive.

What is the unluckiest number?

Four (四 sì), because it sounds like 死 sǐ, death. Combinations such as 14, 74 and 94 are also avoided.

What does 168 mean?

一六八 yī liù bā sounds like 一路發 yī lù fā, 'prosper all the way' — a favourite for businesses and phone numbers.

Do Cantonese and Mandarin readings differ?

Sometimes. 3 sounds like 生 'life' in Cantonese, and 7 has a vulgar slang meaning in Cantonese, while in Mandarin it can mean 'rise' or 'together'.
