Frequently asked questions

How is the lucky number score calculated?

Each digit carries a traditional weight (8 highest, then 6, 9, 2, 3, 7, 1, 0, 5; 4 strongly negative). We add bonuses for famous combinations such as 168, 518, 888 and 8888, subtract for unlucky pairs such as 14 and 74, and normalise to 0-100.

Should I change my business phone number because of a low score?

If a large share of your customers are Chinese-speaking, a number free of 4s and rich in 8s is a cheap, one-time improvement to perception. Run alternatives through the analyzer and ask your carrier which are available.

Why do red envelope amounts avoid the number 4?

Four (四 sì) sounds like death (死 sǐ). Even amounts are preferred for happy occasions, while funerals traditionally use odd amounts in white envelopes.

Is the opening-date scorer the same as a Chinese almanac?

No. It is a transparent heuristic using date digits, the lunar calendar (festivals, Ghost Month), Qingming and weekday traffic. A traditional Tong Shu reading also uses the owners' birth data; our Growth Desk can arrange one.

What makes a numeric domain valuable to Chinese buyers?

Short length, .com extension, repetition, lucky digits (8, 6, 9), no 4, and easy pronunciation in Mandarin and Cantonese.
