What does 738888 mean?

七三八八八八 — 起 · 生 · 發發發發 — "rise, grow, and prosper again and again." Here is the culture, the language and the money behind it.

2. Why homophones rule Chinese number culture

Mandarin and Cantonese have many words that share sounds, so numbers borrow meaning from the words they resemble. Numbers are also the one "script" every Chinese dialect, every generation and every international customer reads identically — which is why brands like 163.com, 360 and 58.com use digits as names, and why numeric web addresses remain a mature asset class in China.

5. How people use lucky numbers every day

  • Prices: ¥88, ¥168, ¥888 menus and packages; real-estate asking prices ending in 8.
  • Addresses & floors: 8th/18th/28th floors command premiums; many buildings skip 4, 14, 24.
  • Dates: weddings and openings on dates rich in 8 or on festival days; Ghost Month (7th lunar month) is avoided.
  • Gifts: red envelopes in even, lucky amounts (88, 168, 888); never 4.
  • Love codes: 520 (我愛你 "I love you"), 1314 (一生一世 "for a lifetime").

6. Other ways to read 738888

  • As 73 + 8888: "起生" (start a life/venture) + endless prosperity — a natural name for launches, startups and new homes.
  • As 7 + 3 + 8888: a playful modern reading of our own — "every day of the week (7), in body, mind and spirit (3), endless prosperity (8888)". Use it as a greeting.
  • Caution: readings are folk traditions; interpretations vary by dialect and region.

Frequently asked questions

Is 738888 a lucky number?

Yes, in traditional Chinese number symbolism it is very favourable: four 8s (發 prosper), a 3 (生 growth) and a 7 (起 rise), with no 4.

Why is 7 considered mixed?

Seven can sound like 起 (rise) or 齊 (together), which is positive, but the 7th lunar month is Ghost Month, and in Cantonese 7 is a vulgar slang word.

Why do Chinese buyers pay so much for 8s?

Eight (八 bā) sounds like 發 fā, to prosper. A number full of 8s is a visible, permanent signal of prosperity on a car, a phone or a storefront, and scarcity pushes prices up.
