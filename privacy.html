Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Summary

Our tools run in your browser — numbers you type into them are not sent to us. We receive personal information only when you submit a form (audit request, contact, pledge, contest entry, application or newsletter).

2. What we collect

  • Form data you provide (name, email, phone, company, message, and optional fields).
  • Technical context attached to forms: page URL, landing page and UTM campaign tags.
  • Cookies/local storage for your theme, consent choice and, if you accept, analytics and advertising.

3. How we use it

To answer your request, deliver the audit or newsletter, run contests, process pledges, evaluate applications, improve the site and prevent spam. We do not sell personal information.

4. Service providers

  • FormSubmit relays form submissions to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts serves typefaces.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) loads only when you click a video.
  • Google AdSense / Google Analytics load only after you accept cookies.

5. Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and/or other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, California CCPA/CPRA), you can request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data and withdraw consent. Use the contact form with topic "Privacy request".

7. Retention

Inquiries: up to 24 months. Applications: 12 months. Contest entries: 12 months after the contest. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

8. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are for adults.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the date above when practices change.
